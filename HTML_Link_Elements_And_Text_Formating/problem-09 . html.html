Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

Students must follow all university rules and regulations. Students should maintain respectful behaviour on campus.

Attendance

Students should attend classes regularly and maintain the required attendance percentage.

Regular attendance is important for academic progress.

Examination

Students must follow examination instructions. Students should carry the required examination materials.

Students should reach the examination hall before the scheduled examination time.

Assignment Submission

Students must submit assignments before the deadline.
Late submissions may be subject to academic rules.

Assignment Deadline: Follow the date provided by the instructor.

Discipline

Students must maintain discipline and respect teachers, staff and other students.

Good discipline creates a positive learning environment.
