Science Notes

Chemistry

Water is represented by H2O.

Carbon dioxide is represented by CO2.

Oxygen is represented by O2.

Sulfuric acid is represented by H2SO4.

Chemical formulas represent substances using symbols and numbers.

Physics

The area of a square can be written as m2.

The volume of a cube can be written as m3.

Speed describes how quickly an object changes its position.

Biology

Biology is the study of living organisms. Cells are the basic structural units of living organisms.

Cell is an important concept in biology.
