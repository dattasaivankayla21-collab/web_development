Breaking News: Annual College Fest 2026 Announced

GLA University Announces Three-Day Annual College Fest

GLA University has officially announced its Annual College Fest 2026. The three-day event will bring together students from different departments and provide opportunities to participate in technical, cultural and entertainment activities.

The event will be conducted at the GLA University campus in Greater Noida. Students will be able to participate in various competitions, cultural programs and other activities organized by the college.

The college fest will begin on 10 October 2026 and will continue for three days. During the event, students can take part in coding competitions, quizzes, dance programs, singing competitions and other activities. The organizers are expecting a large number of student participants.
