realme 10 Pro+ 5G

Powerful Performance. Curved Display. Amazing Camera.

The realme 10 Pro+ 5G is a smartphone designed for users who want powerful performance, a smooth display, good cameras and fast charging.

Overview

The realme 10 Pro+ 5G comes with a MediaTek Dimensity 1080 5G chipset and a 6.7-inch curved display with a 120Hz refresh rate.

Key Features

Processor: MediaTek Dimensity 1080 5G chipset with an octa-core CPU and speeds up to 2.6 GHz.

Display: 6.7-inch FHD+ curved display with a resolution of 2412 × 1080 pixels and a 120Hz refresh rate.

Storage: Available with 128GB or 256GB internal storage and 6GB or 8GB RAM according to the official India specification.

Camera: The phone features a 108MP ProLight main camera, 8MP ultra-wide camera and 2MP macro camera. It also has a 16MP front camera.

Battery: The phone has a 5000mAh typical battery and supports 67W SUPERVOOC charging.

Design and Display

The realme 10 Pro+ features a 120Hz curved vision display. The display measures 6.7 inches and supports FHD+ resolution, 100% DCI-P3 colour gamut and up to 1.07 billion colours.

Camera

The rear camera system consists of a 108MP ProLight camera, an 8MP ultra-wide camera and a 2MP macro camera. The front camera is 16MP.

Battery and Charging

The smartphone has a 5000mAh typical battery and supports 67W SUPERVOOC charging through its USB Type-C port.

Special Features

The realme 10 Pro+ includes 5G connectivity, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.2, dual speakers and an in-display fingerprint sensor.
